FAQ

Questions music brands ask first.

Is there really no upfront development fee?

For approved revenue-share partnerships, yes. PHATSO evaluates brand fit, audience and product viability before agreeing terms.

What plugin formats can PHATSO currently support?

Current PHATSO capability is macOS AU and VST3. Windows and AAX are not promised here unless agreed separately later.

Do we need to design or code the plugin?

No. PHATSO handles the software product side. Your role is brand direction, audience knowledge, feedback and promotion.

Can we choose the plugin idea?

Yes, but the final product direction is shaped together so it fits your brand and has a clear reason to exist.

Do you guarantee approval?

No. Revenue-share projects are selective because PHATSO only wants to build products with a real chance of selling.
